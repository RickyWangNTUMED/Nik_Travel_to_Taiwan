Taiwan Trip Itinerary

August 19 – 21 | Prepared by Wang Yu Hsiang

August 19: Arrival & Luxury Welcome

  • 5:05 AM — Arrival: I arrive at 📍 Taoyuan Airport (TPE). Since you will already be in Taipei, the afternoon is entirely open for an optional, relaxed hangout before dinner.
  • 6:00 PM — Welcome Dinner: Meet up at 📍 Le Palais. We are having an extravagant welcome dinner at this Michelin 3-star Cantonese restaurant, famous for its luxurious tasting menus and iconic flaming Peking duck.
  • Overnight — Xinyi District: You will be staying overnight at my place in Taipei (📍 No. 8, Lane 284, Wuxing St).

August 20: Taichung to Lukang Heritage Tour

  • 9:11 AM — High Speed Rail: Take the HSR from Taipei, arriving at the 📍 Taichung HSR Station at 10:15 AM.
  • 11:00 AM — Luggage Drop: Arrive at the 📍 Lukang Township Northern Visitor Center to drop off your luggage so we can explore unencumbered.
  • 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM — Loose Walking Route: Taking a relaxed, north-to-south stroll through the historic sights without a strict timeline. We will hit:
    • 📍 Lukang Mazu Temple: Starting here to admire the ornate architecture.
    • 📍 Jiuqu Lane: Wandering south through historic wind-blocking brick paths.
    • Lunch & Snacks: Grabbing local street food like oyster omelets or steamed buns around the old streets.
    • 📍 Mo Ru Lane (Gentlemen Alley): Squeezing through the famously narrow historic alleyway.
    • 📍 Lukang Lung-shan Temple: Concluding the southern end of the walk at this beautifully preserved Buddhist temple before taking a leisurely walk back north.
  • 5:30 PM — Departure: Meet back at the Visitor Center to collect your luggage and take a taxi to Changhua.
  • Evening — Grandma's House: Arrive at my grandma's place (📍 No. 159, Shijian Rd, Changhua) for dinner and to stay for the night.

August 21: Changhua Sights & Taichung Michelin Dessert

  • 8:30 AM — Baguashan Morning: Head up the hill to explore the 📍 Baguashan Scenic Area. We'll walk the skybridge, see the massive 22-meter statue at the Eight Trigram Mountains Buddha Landscape, and visit the Visitor Center.
  • 11:00 AM — A-San Ba-wan: Head into Changhua City to try the famous, homestyle crispy fried ba-wan (meatballs) at 📍 A-San Ba-wan.
  • 12:00 PM — MINIMAL Ice Cream: Head to Taichung for world-renowned ice cream at 📍 MINIMAL. Note: They generally open at 1:00 PM on Fridays, so we might grab a nearby coffee while waiting for the doors to open.
  • 3:00 PM — Airport Connection: Catch the HSR from Taichung, arriving at Taoyuan by 3:36 PM. Transfer to the MRT to arrive at the airport around 4:00 PM for your 6:20 PM flight to Osaka.

Safe travels! Looking forward to an amazing trip.
